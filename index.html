Daily operating rhythm

The same four steps, every working day

Source: Discussion Pack.

1. Prepare

Review today's meetings, new leads, overdue tasks and follow-ups. Done when everyone has a short priority list and every overdue task has an owner.

2. Execute channel work

Specialist 1 contacts approved companies. Specialist 2 contacts partners and chases referrals. Done when all attempts are logged in the CRM the same day.

3. Run meetings, progress deals

Specialist 3 and the owner run discovery and demos, record fit, need, objections and next step, and prepare proposals. Capacity is confirmed first.

4. Close the day

Update stages, replies, proposals, wins and losses, and tomorrow's actions. Specialist 3 publishes the dashboard.

    White-label approval gate (proposed)

    1. At least 3 paying customer accounts sourced by the partner.
    2. A credible 90-day sales forecast with named target accounts.
    3. A named sales owner and customer-support owner.
    4. Agreed payment, renewal, support and escalation processes.
    5. No unresolved disputes over customer ownership, IP, compliance or payments.
    6. A written agreement covering branding, licence rights, pricing, data access, hosting, service levels, termination and customer migration.
    Recommended start: run the 30-day test with non-exclusive referral and reseller arrangements. Measure partner quality and real demand before committing to white-label development, exclusive territories or extra subscriptions.

    Compliance and decisions before launch

    Launch scope decision: the supplied source material contains UK and USA references. Confirm the primary direct-outbound geography by Day 15 and apply the relevant outreach rules before launch.

    Rules depend on the market. The steps below come from the Discussion Pack and are the UK version (TPS, ICO). Replace them with the local rules for each market you pick (for example CAN-SPAM and TCPA for the USA, or GDPR for Europe) and have them reviewed before launch.

    Outreach compliance (UK version)

    1. Assign an owner to approve contact rules and the suppression process.
    2. Screen live marketing calls against CTPS/TPS and the internal do-not-contact list.
    3. Identify whether each prospect is a limited company or an individual or sole trader.
    4. Document the lawful basis and notice for personal data.
    5. Include sender identity and a working opt-out where required.
    6. Review current ICO guidance before launch.
